Classroom Control & Discipline

  • Proactive Engagement: Keep lessons fast-paced with interactive questioning to prevent downtime.
  • Non-Verbal Cues: Use eye contact, proximity, and hand gestures rather than shouting to regain attention.
  • Clear Routine: Establish entry, seating, and transition procedures from Day 1.
🧠

Types of Students & Profiles

  • The Visual Learner: Learns best through diagrams, charts, and colored notes.
  • The Auditory Learner: Thrives on verbal instructions, read-alouds, and discussions.
  • The Kinesthetic Learner: Needs physical movement and hands-on manipulative practice.
💡

Problem-Solving Strategies

  • Root-Cause Analysis: Is poor performance due to comprehension gaps or home stress? Speak privately.
  • Differentiated Tasks: Break complex problems into smaller, scaffolded steps.
  • Peer Tutoring: Pair high-performing pupils with peers needing reinforcement.
